## 2026-10-26 — §10.2, formula for the roots $a_\pm$

Source location: `levy_qsd_restart.tex` line 1147.

Corrected a sign in the displayed formula for the roots $a_\pm$ in §10.2. As printed, $a_\pm=\mp\eta+[(5-2\eta)\pm\sqrt{(5-2\eta)^2+32\kappa_\infty/\eta^2}]/2$ gives $a_-\approx-1.309$. The correct formula is $a_\pm=\mp\eta+[\pm(5-2\eta)+\sqrt{(5-2\eta)^2+32\kappa_\infty/\eta^2}]/2$, which gives the stated values $a_+=4.42681549\ldots$ and $a_-=3.19141579\ldots$ and $a_+-a_-=5-4\eta$.

Only the display is affected: the stated numerical values of $a_\pm$, and everything computed from them, are correct. The archived PDF, version DOI and repository tag are unchanged; the erratum is recorded here and at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/levy-qsd-exponential-moments-restart/>.
